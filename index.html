What is CockroachAI ($ROACH) and its core mission? ▼

CockroachAI is an autonomous, decentralized utility protocol deployed on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20). Built for ultimate market resilience, it powers three core pillars: the Sentinel AI Scanner (real-time bytecode vulnerability and honeypot detection), RoachSwap DEX (anti-MEV private order routing with a permanent 50% fee buyback-and-burn mechanism), and Deflationary Staking Vaults delivering up to 148% APY.

Do I get $ROACH in my wallet immediately after buying? ▼

Yes! Upon transaction confirmation in the presale widget, $ROACH tokens are transferred directly to your connected Web3 wallet (MetaMask, Trust Wallet, or Binance Web3 Wallet). To shield early investors from front-running snipers and premature DEX dumping, public trading remains locked until official launch. However, your tokens can be deposited immediately into the Staking Vaults to begin compounding yield right away.

How does the 3M / 6M / 12M staking APY work? ▼

Staking rewards are distributed from an immutable, dedicated 200,000,000 $ROACH vault (20% of total supply). Returns are tailored to lock duration:

• 3 Months Lock: 36% APY (9% Net Yield on principal)

• 12 Months Lock: 148% APY (148% Net Yield + 2x multiplier & zero RoachSwap platform fees)

Which currencies are accepted and what are the network requirements? ▼

You can participate using either BNB or USDT (BEP-20). Make sure your wallet is set to the BNB Smart Chain (Chain ID: 56) network and keep a small balance of BNB (0.005–0.01 BNB) to cover BSC network gas fees.

How does the 10% instant referral commission work? ▼

Once your wallet is connected, your custom referral link is generated instantly. Whenever someone contributes to the presale using your link, the smart contract automatically routes 10% of their contribution directly to your wallet in the same transaction—no withdrawal claims or waiting periods required.

Are the smart contracts audited and secure? ▼

Yes. The contract architecture is built using OpenZeppelin v5 standards with SafeERC20 and nonReentrant execution guards. Bytecode static analysis via Slither yielded zero high or medium severity vulnerabilities, and full state transitions have passed 100% of EVM unit tests in Foundry.

When will $ROACH be listed on PancakeSwap and unlocked for trading? ▼

Immediately upon conclusion of Stage 1 and subsequent presale rounds, liquidity will be seeded and permanently locked on PancakeSwap. Trading will be unlocked globally via a one-way immutable switch, enabling instant trading without manual claim fees.
